# EL SCORE OS --- Reporting, Search & Notifications

## Reporting

One Reports capability should support role-aware reports with relevant
filters.

General filters where applicable:

-   date
-   month
-   department
-   employee
-   role
-   status
-   student
-   tutor
-   mentor
-   Sales Counsellor
-   subject
-   grade
-   curriculum
-   lead source

Only relevant filters should appear for a report.

## Notifications

Notifications should be action-based and link to the relevant
record/action.

Examples:

-   overdue Sales follow-up
-   Demo requiring Sales follow-up
-   Student awaiting Mentor assignment
-   Student awaiting Tutor assignment
-   missing class update
-   Mentor verification pending
-   Finance verification pending
-   renewal approaching
-   meeting invitation
-   meeting task assigned
-   overdue task

Avoid notification spam. Notifications should represent actionable
state.
